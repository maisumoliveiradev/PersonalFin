# SDD-037 --- Ownership Transfer

## Objective

Let the Owner transfer ownership to an active member (FR-004, DR-009).

## Scope

-   API: transfer (with explicit confirmation); the previous Owner
    becomes an Administrator member; audited.
-   Client: transfer action in "Membros".

## Acceptance

-   There is exactly one Owner before and after; the new Owner has every
    permission immediately.

## Definition of Done

OpenAPI, tests, journey, docs, changelog.
